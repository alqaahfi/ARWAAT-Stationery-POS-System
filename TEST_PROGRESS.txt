STAGE 1 DECISIONS
  D1 (rule 1) Editing a saved bill: a cashier may keep the bill's OWN stored price for a pack size (same
     customer only); any other price must match the price engine. Changing the customer re-prices.
  D2 (rule 1) Admin manual price override is in the quick Sale form (BillLines price box, Admin only).
     The keyboard POS has no price box (unchanged); Admin uses the quick Sale form to override.
  D3 (rule 3) Pack-size conversion factors (e.g. Box = 12) are not quantities typed at the counter and stay as
     they are. Old saved rows with decimal quantities are left untouched (they still display and count).
  D4 (rule 5) Linked payments of a voided bill are MARKED voided (payments.is_voided) rather than excluded by a
     join; every payment sum/list filters is_voided = 0. Voided bills are hidden from Home "Recent sales" and from
     customer ledger timelines (zero effect), but stay visible (flagged) in Sales History and POS Today's bills.
  D5 (rule 5) A return against a voided bill cannot be created locally; if two PCs void and return the same bill
     at the same moment, sync keeps both (left visible, like other sync conflicts).
  D6 (rule 6) Buying-price write-back from Add Stock / purchases is not warned (not in the rule's list: product
     form + Admin price override). Percentage-over-cost pricing can never be below cost.
  D7 (rule 11) Key for "Add new item" is F7 (F6 = Return). Quick-add is in the keyboard POS only (not the quick
     Sale overlay). Merge needs a target pack size with the same pieces per pack as the item was sold in (Piece = 1);
     the item's old stock-movement rows stay on the (switched-off) item and its stock moves across as two movements;
     purchase lines are moved on this PC only (purchase_items are insert-only in sync - money is unaffected).
  D8 (rule 11) "Buying price missing" = item still needs review OR its pack size has buying price 0. Such items are
     left out of stock value and their sales are reported separately in P&L instead of counting a zero cost silently.
  D9 (rule 12) An approval covers one save (everything that save needed) and is spent only when the save succeeds;
     it is bound to the window that asked and expires after 3 minutes. The Admin never needs the box: selling without
     stock as Admin is a plain "Sell anyway?" confirm (still put on Stock to recount, not in the approvals log).
     A cashier can never change another cashier's bill, even with approval (own bills only).
  D10 (rule 13) The 10 minutes count from the bill's created_at (first save); editing does not restart the window.
     A computer clock set earlier than the bill counts as inside the window (the wrong-clock banner, rule 15, warns).
  D11 (rule 14) "Original" = the first SUCCESSFUL print; a preview never counts. Payment receipts and purchase
     receipts are not stamped (the rule is about bills).
  D12 (rule 17) In a no-bill return, the 30-day lowest selling price accounts for bill-level discounts
     (unit_price * total_amount / subtotal) so a customer never gets back more than the lowest discounted unit price
     charged in the last 30 days. No-bill returns are charged to the Admin who recorded them in Cashier Performance.
  D13 (anomalies) Large quantity (> 100) or line total (> Rs 100,000) asks for confirmation both when adding/editing
     a line in the POS and on the server (saveSale returns needsLargeConfirm unless allowLargeQty is true).
  D14 (anomalies) Signing out (or session ending) while a non-empty POS cart is open dispatches pos:before-logout,
     which automatically holds the cart into pos_held_bills_<station> and clears the draft; closing/reloading the
     window flushes any pending debounced draft immediately on beforeunload.
