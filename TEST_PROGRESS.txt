THINGS THAT DIFFER FROM THE TEST BRIEF (the app does not have these features)
- Activation / 500 key pool: does not exist. Section 1 = NOT APPLICABLE.
- Tax (5%): does not exist (tax is always 0).  Item-level discount: does not exist (bill discount only).
- Opening cash / cash-in-hand screen: does not exist (cash was to be computed from the database).
- Customer credit limit: stored but NOT enforced.        -> needs your decision
- Barcodes belong to a product Type, not to a pack size, so Box barcodes 1002 / 1005 cannot exist.  -> needs your decision
- Decimal quantities are allowed for every item (no "whole numbers only" flag).                      -> needs your decision
- Voiding a sale is a hard delete (history stays only in the activity log).
- The two apps are ONE process with two windows, not two processes.
- Deleting customers/suppliers does not exist (only Deactivate).
